## Review and limitations

The generator validates counts, four distinct nonempty options, valid indices, source references and exact stem uniqueness. It does not fetch content at runtime. Mathematical alternatives preserve signs, decimal points and superscript/subscript digits because those distinctions matter to answer correctness.

A manual pass corrected potentially overlapping disease-transmission distractors, overlapping deficiency effects and overly broad body-location alternatives. An independent agent read all 1,000 science stems and keyed answers, identified seven concrete overlapping-function distractors (neuron, vitamin B12, zinc, parenchyma, radicle, onion bulb and ginger rhizome), and those options were replaced. Instrument articles were also corrected. Sources are shown with explanations so a learner can check a fact. Disease questions are educational knowledge checks and do not prescribe diagnosis or treatment. Notes retain complete short explanations rather than splitting at periods in names or initials.

The current bank has a substantial sports component, including Paris Paralympics event and medal knowledge, alongside science, diplomacy, climate, heritage and law. It is a dated revision bank rather than a daily news feed. An individual web audit of every one of the 1,350 question records has not been performed; the recorded research and independent sample review should not be described as that.

The coordinating agent independently read all 350 current-affairs stems and keys. Repeated Preethi Pal sport-recall prompts were replaced with sourced Indian Paralympic participation figures (84 athletes, 12 sports); two medal stems were corrected grammatically; one reciprocal ISS prompt was replaced by the experiments' microgravity environment; and the August 2025 milestone was specified as the launch-pad foundation within the SSLV complex. IDs and exact counts were preserved.

The coordinating agent's spelling/source audit corrected Pranav Soorma's name against the official IPC club-throw F51 results, now linked on his records. The independent science reviewer rechecked all seven distractor fixes and reported no further factual blockers. The centrifuge stem was revised to ask its use rather than what it measures.
