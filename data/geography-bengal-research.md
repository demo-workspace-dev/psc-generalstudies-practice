# Geography and Bengal content preparation

Prepared on 1 November 2026. These files belong to the separate reading app; existing practice papers were not edited.

## Authorship and scope

The reproducible authoring source is `tools/geography_bengal_build.py`. Curated fact tables create distinct questions about different factual attributes; environmental and physical-geography concepts have individually written definitions. The bank does not create option permutations or paraphrases to multiply the count. A deterministic selection across the complete candidate order keeps all topic groups represented. Questions with their complete answer already present in the stem are excluded before selection.

- Geography: **750 questions**, **589 concise notes**; physical geography, atmosphere, oceans, environment, India, rivers, dams, parks, mountain systems, world capitals, agriculture, minerals, straits and canals.
- West Bengal: **500 questions**, **307 concise notes**; districts, landscapes, rivers, places, culture, literature, concrete personal contributions, chronology, industries and institutions.

## Research actually performed

Parallel MCP web searches were run in this session for NIOS/NCERT geography and history, official West Bengal district and tourism pages, UNESCO heritage entries, Bengali literature, Ramakrishna Mission, INSA scientific biographies, ISI history, BFI cinema information, capitals, maritime routes, and environmental conventions. Sources in each JSON are those consulted as relevant references, with more specific records attached to the corresponding source families.

Relevant pages opened with Parallel fetch: [Sundarbans National Park](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/452/), [Santiniketan](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1375/), [Cooch Behar royal history](https://coochbehar.gov.in/about-district/history/), [Bankura tourism](https://www.wbtourism.gov.in/district/detail/Bankura), and [Ramsar Convention background](https://www.ramsar.org/about-convention-wetlands). Fetch attempts for two NCERT PDFs did not return extracted content; their search excerpts and the NIOS course references were available.

Important discrepancy: a West Bengal tourism page gives an incorrect Sundarbans inscription year. UNESCO's World Heritage List records **1987**, which is the date used here. Cooch Behar's merger into West Bengal is anchored to the district administration's **1 January 1950** record. Santiniketan's inscription is **2023** and Durga Puja in Kolkata's intangible-heritage inscription is **2021**.

## Review and practical limits

Structural checks cover exact counts, stable IDs, four unique choices, valid answer indices, resolved source references, distinct stems, balanced shuffled answer positions, and answer-in-stem clues. Manual review removed broad national-park habitat questions that allowed several true descriptions, generic personal-field questions, overlapping narrow literary genres, mixed-level river outlets, and self-evident place-name questions. Multi-state location alternatives are filtered to avoid presenting a containing state as a competing answer.

An independent history/polity author subsequently identified same-name Pandua and Madan Mohan Temple ambiguity, overlapping river-source/region options, and multi-metal minerals. Those were resolved by qualifying the named places, curating geographically disjoint river alternatives, and asking which metal an ore is **principally mined for**, with metal-only options. The final counts remain 750 and 500.

The source links are educational references, not a claim that all 1,250 questions received a separate line-by-line online audit. Original factual records were compiled and checked against broad references plus selected specific claims. The learner should treat these as a broad revision bank; further editorial review can improve the depth of the brief table-based explanations. No late-2026 news, unstable officeholders, district-count claim, or undated production ranking is used in these sections.
