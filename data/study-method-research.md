# Syllabus and learning-method references

Researched with Parallel MCP web search and opened with its web-fetch tool on 1 November 2026.

* WBPSC, Clerkship Examination 2023 advertisement and Scheme & Syllabus, General Studies paragraph: https://psc.wb.gov.in/Download?param1=An_20231204154303_Clerkship.pdf&param2=advertisement . The official paragraph covers everyday observation/science, current events and problems with special reference to India, and elementary Indian history/geography. Polity, economy, environment and Bengal are included here as useful general-knowledge extensions following the existing practice guide. This app is a learning companion, not an official mock examination or an assertion about a changed 2026 syllabus.
* Dunlosky & Rawson (2015), *Practice Tests, Spaced Practice, and Successive Relearning*: https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/features/stl-0000024.pdf . Opened text supports retrieval practice, delayed tests, spacing, and combining practice tests across sessions.
* Latimier, Peyre & Ramus (2021), *A Meta-Analytic Review of the Benefit of Spacing out Retrieval Practice Episodes on Retention*, ERIC record: https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1310148 . Opened abstract reports benefit of spaced retrieval over massed retrieval, without a general significant advantage for expanding over uniform schedules. Consequently, the toolkit labels its day 1/3/7/14/30 schedule as a practical example, not an optimal research-prescribed algorithm.

The mnemonics, comparison notebook, map sketches and daily routine are practical study suggestions. The app does not claim they are equally supported by the research or guarantee an examination result. Subject-specific source verification and limitations are recorded by the content authors in the other research files.
